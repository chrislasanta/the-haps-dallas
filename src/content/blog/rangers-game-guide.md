I have been to a lot of baseball stadiums, and Globe Life Field genuinely ranks among the very best. It opened in 2020 (right as the world was shutting down — rough timing), but getting to experience it with a full crowd now is just *chef's kiss*. If you haven't been, this is the year.

## Getting There

- **Address:** 734 Stadium Dr, Arlington, TX 76011
- **By car:** Plenty of parking garages on-site and nearby lots. The official Rangers lots are convenient but pricier ($25–$40). Third-party lots along Division St are often half that.
- **By train:** The TEXRail commuter line connects DFW Airport to Globe Life Field — great option if you're coming from Dallas proper and don't want to deal with post-game traffic.

## Tickets

- **Budget pick:** Look for games against smaller-market teams on weeknights — you can often find great seats in the $25–$40 range.
- **Best experience:** The Delta SKY360° Club is worth a splurge at least once. All-inclusive food and drinks, incredible sightlines, air-conditioned. Absolute luxury.
- **App:** Download the Texas Rangers app before you go — mobile tickets and express lanes make entry a breeze.

## What to Eat

The food at Globe Life is genuinely good. Some highlights:

- **Texas Brisket Dog** — it's exactly what it sounds like and it's incredible
- **Lockhart Smokehouse** — real legit BBQ inside a baseball stadium. Texas, baby.
- **Churro dog** — a dessert churro in a sugar cone with ice cream. Absurd and wonderful.
- **Local craft beer section** — solid selection of Texas breweries on tap

## Pro Tips

- **Arrive 30 minutes early** — the pre-game atmosphere is great and you'll avoid the rush through security
- **Bring a light jacket** — even in summer, the stadium AC can be intense
- **Stay for the whole game** — the post-win fireworks show is genuinely worth it
- **Check the bobblehead schedule** — they do promo giveaway nights throughout the season that are super fun
