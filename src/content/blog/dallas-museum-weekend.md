Real talk: the Dallas Arts District is one of the most underrated things about living here. It's the largest contiguous urban arts district in the entire country, and we basically have it in our backyard. I'm on a personal mission to get more people down there, so here we go.

## The Dallas Museum of Art

Free general admission, every single day. That alone should have you heading downtown. The permanent collection is excellent — strong on American art, European masters, and a fantastic decorative arts section. But the special exhibitions are where it gets exciting; they've been bringing in some truly world-class shows recently.

- **Address:** 1717 N Harwood St
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 11am–5pm, Thu until 9pm
- **Cost:** Free general admission; special exhibitions vary

## Nasher Sculpture Center

Okay, the Nasher is genuinely one of my favorite places in all of Dallas. The indoor/outdoor sculpture garden is absolutely beautiful — I've been there probably 15 times and I always see something new. They also have a fantastic rotating roster of special exhibitions.

- **Address:** 2001 Flora St
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 11am–5pm
- **Cost:** $10 adults / $7 students & seniors / Free for members

## Perot Museum of Nature and Science

This one is more "wow" than "contemplative." The building itself is stunning (it looks like a giant cube hovering over the street), and inside it's genuinely thrilling — especially the energy hall and the dinosaur exhibits. If you have kids, this is a must. If you don't have kids, it's still a must.

- **Address:** 2201 N Field St
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 10am–5pm, Sun 11am–5pm
- **Cost:** $25 adults / $18 kids; buy tickets online in advance to skip the line

## The Perfect Arts District Day

Here's how I'd do it:

1. **Morning:** Grab coffee at The Corner (right next to the DMA) and walk the Nasher garden first — it's best in the morning light.
2. **Midday:** Head into the DMA for a couple hours. Hit the café for lunch.
3. **Afternoon:** Walk over to the Perot for the energy and science exhibits.
4. **Evening:** End at Sixty Vines or Meso Maya nearby for dinner — you've earned it.

Dallas is truly a great city. We just have to actually *use* it!
