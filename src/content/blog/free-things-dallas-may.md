One of the things I love most about Dallas is that you absolutely do not need to spend a lot of money to have a fantastic time. Here are 10 completely free (or nearly free) things you can do this week.

## 1. White Rock Lake

Rent a kayak or just walk/run/bike the 9-mile trail around the lake. It's beautiful, it's free, and it genuinely feels like you've left the city. The bath house has a small café if you need a snack.

![Peaceful lake with walking trail and trees](/images/inline-white-rock-lake.jpg)
*White Rock Lake feels like a secret escape hidden right inside the city.*

## 2. Klyde Warren Park Free Fitness

Saturday and Sunday mornings, free yoga and fitness classes on the Great Lawn. No registration needed, just show up. Mats recommended but not required.

## 3. Dallas Arboretum (Free Thursdays)

Okay, technically the Arboretum normally costs money, but Dallas residents get free admission on select Thursdays. Check their website — it rotates. Even at full price it's worth it, but free is better.

## 4. Bishop Arts District Walk

Just walking around Bishop Arts is genuinely fun. Window shopping, street art, great people-watching. Pop into shops without feeling obligated to buy. Grab a $3 coffee from Casa del Agua while you're there.

## 5. Katy Trail

Five miles of paved trail connecting Uptown to Highland Park. Great for walking, running, or biking. There's something really alive about it on weekend mornings.

![Tree-lined urban trail with joggers and cyclists](/images/inline-katy-trail.jpg)
*Katy Trail on a Sunday morning is peak Dallas — bring your walking shoes and your best playlist.*

## 6. Dallas Farmers Market (Saturday)

Technically free to walk around. You'll probably end up buying fresh produce or a pastry, but that's a feature, not a bug. The Saturday market is the biggest.

## 7. The Perot Museum (First Tuesday of the Month)

Free admission the first Tuesday of each month from 5–8pm. It gets busy, but it's worth it. 

## 8. Deep Ellum Street Art Tour

Deep Ellum has some of the best murals in Texas. Just walk around — there's constantly new art going up. Katy Turner's mural on Commerce is a personal favorite.

## 9. Lower Greenville Library Events

The Dallas Public Library branches all have free programming. The SMU/Lower Greenville area branch frequently has author readings and workshops that are legitimately interesting.

## 10. Reverchon Park (Uptown)

Underrated, quiet, and beautiful. Great for a picnic. Bring snacks, a blanket, and a good book.

---

See? Dallas has SO much going on ALL OF THE TIME. No excuses — get out there!
